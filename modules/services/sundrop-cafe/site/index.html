Entry Instructions

  1. Find the number pad at the gate to the left of the Marquis on Gaston leasing office.
  2. Enter 4909# and wait for a long beep.
  3. Enter through the gate.
  4. Walk straight, past the pool and down the stone path.
  5. Turn right after passing the pool.
  6. Enter the building directly ahead.
  7. Go up to floor 3.
  8. Sundrop Cafe is number 1439.

Parking Instructions

Easy (Limited Parking)

  1. Park in the parking lot of the Marquis on Gaston leasing office.
  2. Register your car at app.apartmentpermits.com/guest — Property: Marquis on Gaston, Unit: 1439, PIN: 8697.
  3. Follow the walking entry instructions above.

Hard (More Parking)

  1. Go to the Marquis on Gaston entry gate located on N Malcolm X Blvd.
  2. Enter 4909# and wait for a long beep as the gate opens.
  3. Continue driving forward. You will pass 3 parking deck entrances to your right. Enter the third (last) one.
  4. Go up to the top floor, and park in the uncovered visitor parking spaces.
  5. Register your car at app.apartmentpermits.com/guest — Property: Marquis on Gaston, Unit: 1439, PIN: 8697.
  6. Enter the middle building to the northeast. 1439 will be on the right at the end of the hall.
